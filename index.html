How this garden works

One small smart contract, three clear actions.

01 / ADOPT

Name your crop

Your wallet creates a new plot record and becomes its owner.

02 / EXPLORE

Read the record

Plot names, owners, and updates can be looked up by anyone.

03 / TEND

Share a milestone

The owner can record a new status, with every change visible on-chain.

A transparent little garden

Records live on Sepolia, Ethereum’s public test network. This is a virtual farming demonstration, not a claim about physical land or crop verification.
